Embed Double Data's proxy network in your application with a lightweight SDK for Android and desktop. Users choose whether to share their connection, and your app stays in control of the session.

## Integrate in four steps

1. [Download](/sdk/downloads) the package for your platform.
2. Create a client with your application configuration and privacy notice.
3. Show the built-in consent screen when the user chooses to enable sharing.
4. Display sharing status and provide a withdrawal control.

After acceptance, proxy requests run automatically. Declining keeps sharing off. Withdrawal stops active connections and prevents reconnection.
