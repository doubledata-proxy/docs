---
title: "Participation flow"
description: "Present the Double Data notice in your app and keep sharing under the user's control."
---

Double Data supplies the notice. You control its layout, typography, and native presentation. Use the complete notice returned by the SDK; do not replace its content with a checkbox or an acceptance flag.

## Ask once per session

1. The user chooses **Enable sharing** in your application.
2. Your built-in or custom presenter displays the purpose, complete text, operator, contact, and privacy link.
3. **Enable sharing** records acceptance and permits the session to start. **Decline**, dismissal, or cancellation leaves sharing off.

Individual proxy requests need no further prompts. Keep your app's unrelated features available after decline.

## Keep controls available

Display sharing status and offer **Pause** and **Withdraw consent**. Set session duration and traffic limits in [configuration](/sdk/configuration). Pause stops traffic; resuming still requires current consent and remaining quota.

Withdrawal invalidates permission immediately, stops active work, and blocks reconnection. A new session requires another affirmative choice. Close the client when its owner exits, and await cleanup off the UI thread before creating a replacement.

## Use your own screen

Android uses `ConsentPresenter`; desktop uses the C consent callback; Swift uses a cancellable consent prompt. Present the supplied JSON with your app's styling and return acceptance only after the person's explicit action. Cancellation must dismiss the screen and release any waiting callback.

The SDK records the notice version and content hash with consent. These are receipt metadata, not participant-facing copy. [Current notice](/sdk/notices/2026-10-28.1/en.json) · [Privacy](/sdk/privacy).
